Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

In short

  • Everything you enter in TapSum stays on your iPhone.
  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal data.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking in the app.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Data you enter in the app

TapSum lets you record expenses, income, accounts, budgets, subscriptions, categories and notes. All of this information is stored only locally on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server, and we have no way to see it.

If you have iCloud Backup or computer backups turned on, your device backup may include TapSum's data. Those backups are created and managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us.

You can delete all of your TapSum data at any time by deleting the app from your device.

In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions in TapSum are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment card details or billing address. The app only receives confirmation from Apple that a purchase is active, so it can unlock the features you paid for. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of TapSum contains no analytics tools, no advertising and no third-party tracking. We do not use your data to track you across other apps or websites.

If we add analytics or attribution services in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released, list the services used and the data they process, and ask for your consent where the law or Apple requires it.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing support. We keep this correspondence only as long as needed to handle your request and do not share it with anyone.

Children

TapSum is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Because the app does not collect data, no data about children is collected either.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Since your financial data never leaves your device, you control it directly in the app. For anything related to emails you have sent us, write to gsumtap@gmail.com and we will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Significant changes, such as adding analytics, will also be mentioned in the App Store release notes.
