About TapSum

TapSum is a simple expense tracker for iPhone. You record expenses and income by hand, organize them into accounts and categories, and set monthly or yearly budgets. Everything stays on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All of your data is kept locally on your iPhone. It is not sent to any server, and no one else can see it.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Your TapSum data is included in your device backup. If you have iCloud Backup or a computer backup turned on and you restore your new iPhone from that backup, your data comes across with it.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and select TapSum to change or cancel it.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it, open TapSum, and use Restore Purchases in the app's settings.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its data. There is no copy anywhere else.
